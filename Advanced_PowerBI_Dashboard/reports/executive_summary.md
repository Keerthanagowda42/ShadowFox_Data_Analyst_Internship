### Key Executive Observations:
1. **Exponential Scaling Phase (2022 -> 2023)**:
   - Revenue expanded by **+96.38%**, nearly doubling volume as new territory distribution hubs in the UK, Germany, and Australia came online.
2. **Sustained Mature Growth (2023 -> 2024)**:
   - Revenue advanced another **+24.29%**, reaching **$1.32M in FY 2024**.
3. **Impeccable Margin Discipline**:
   - Despite rapid geographic scaling, the operating profit margin remained locked between **37.99% and 38.82%**, proving that revenue growth was not bought through aggressive discounting.

## 4. Operational Bottlenecks: Logistics & Delivery SLA

While top-line growth is stellar, operations present a clear risk:
- **On-Time Delivery SLA**: Currently sitting at **68.83%**, falling significantly short of the corporate target of **85.0%**.
- **Shipping Mode Breakdown**:
  - `Same Day`: 92.4% SLA pass rate (0.8 days avg).
  - `First Class`: 78.6% SLA pass rate (2.1 days avg).
  - `Second Class`: 65.4% SLA pass rate (3.2 days avg).
  - `Standard Class`: **59.2% SLA pass rate** (5.1 days avg).
- **Core Diagnosis**: The 3PL carrier network handling Standard Class ground freight suffers persistent fulfillment delays during Q4 holiday volume surges.
