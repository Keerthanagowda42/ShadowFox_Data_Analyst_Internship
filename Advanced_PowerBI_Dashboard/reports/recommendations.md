# Strategic Roadmap & Operational Optimization Recommendations

**Project**: Advanced Level - Executive Business Intelligence Dashboard Using Power BI  
**Author**: ShadowFox Data Analyst Intern  
**Target Stakeholders**: Chief Operating Officer (COO), VP of Supply Chain, Head of Commercial Sales  
**Date**: October 2026  

---

## 1. Executive Summary of Recommendations

The data demonstrates strong financial health ($2.93M revenue, 38.4% profit margin), but reveals critical logistical vulnerabilities (68.8% On-Time Delivery compliance). To maintain growth in FY 2025 while safeguarding customer satisfaction, leadership must enact strategic interventions across four operational pillars.

---

## 2. Recommendation Pillar I: Overhaul 3PL Standard Freight Carrier Contracts

### Findings:
- Standard Class delivery is chosen for **58% of all shipments**, but achieves only a **59.2% on-time delivery rate**, dragging the corporate SLA down to **68.83%**.
- Customers experiencing late deliveries exhibit a **2.3x higher return rate** compared to on-time deliveries.

### Operational Actions:
1. **Renegotiate Regional Service Level Agreements (SLAs)**:
   - Institute financial chargebacks for carriers whose on-time rate drops below 85% during peak periods.
2. **Dynamic Carrier Allocation Engine**:
   - Integrate an automated multi-carrier dispatch tool that switches Standard shipments to secondary regional courier networks when primary carriers exceed capacity.

### Target Impact:
Elevate consolidated On-Time Delivery SLA from **68.8% to $\ge 82.0\%$** within 180 days.

---

## 3. Recommendation Pillar II: Capitalize on High-Margin Office Supplies Bundles

### Findings:
- Office Supplies produces the highest segment profit margin (**41.2%**), but commands only **21.5%** of gross sales.
- B2B Corporate buyers purchasing Technology equipment rarely add office consumables during the same checkout.

### Operational Actions:
1. **Automated B2B Workstation Bundles**:
   - Package commercial laser printers with 6 months of multipurpose paper and high-yield toner at an attractive 10% bundle discount.
2. **Corporate Auto-Replenishment Program**:
   - Introduce automated standing orders for presentation binders and paper reams with Net-30 invoicing.

### Target Impact:
Expand Office Supplies revenue share from **21.5% to 26.0%**, injecting an estimated **+$65,000 in net profit**.

---

## 4. Recommendation Pillar III: Return Mitigation for Heavy Machines

### Findings:
- The overall return rate is healthy at **4.31%**, but **Commercial Copiers and Large Machines** exhibit return rates exceeding **7.8%**, representing over **$42,000 in freight and restocking loss**.
- Post-return feedback reveals configuration complexity as the primary driver for returns.

### Operational Actions:
1. **Mandatory White-Glove Onboarding**:
   - Provide a complimentary 30-minute virtual onboarding session with an enterprise setup specialist for any equipment purchase over $1,000.
2. **Enhanced Digital Manuals & Unboxing Videos**:
   - Embed QR codes on shipping cartons linking directly to video setup guides.

### Target Impact:
Reduce heavy machine return rates from **7.8% to $<4.0\%$**, recovering ~$22,000 annually.
